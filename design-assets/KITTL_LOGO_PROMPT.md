# Built True Workshop — Kittl logo prompt

## Copy-ready prompt

Design a premium, timeless **horizontal combination logo** for a woodworking education and tool-guidance brand named **BUILT TRUE WORKSHOP**.

The brand promise is practical craftsmanship, trustworthy guidance, precision, confidence, and buying tools for a reason—not hype. The identity should feel established and credible enough for a respected woodworking magazine, but clean and contemporary enough for a modern digital publication.

Create an original, simple symbol inspired by **traditional wood joinery and accurate layout**: two solid geometric wood forms meeting in a precise mortise-and-tenon, bridle joint, or dovetail-like connection. The negative space may subtly suggest a **T**, a carpenter’s square, or a check for “true,” but do not force a complicated BT monogram. The symbol must remain instantly recognizable at 24–40 pixels.

Pair the symbol with a strong wordmark:

- **BUILT TRUE** — dominant, confident slab-serif or sturdy editorial serif, uppercase or small caps.
- **WORKSHOP** — smaller utilitarian sans serif beneath it, widely tracked.
- Preserve the exact spelling: **BUILT TRUE WORKSHOP**.

Color palette:

- Deep walnut: `#241A15`
- Forest green: `#2F5B4B`
- Workshop amber accent: `#D47A1F`
- Warm cream background: `#FAF7F0`

Keep the design flat and vector-friendly. It must work in full color, one color, reversed white, and as a standalone icon. Use bold shapes, clean spacing, and a balanced silhouette. Provide horizontal, stacked, and icon-only lockups on transparent backgrounds.

Avoid circular saw blades, crossed hammers or axes, shields, mountains, cabins, trees, mascots, distressed “craft beer” styling, fake wood grain, gradients, bevels, shadows, thin lines, ornate badges, generic construction-company logos, illegible initials, and mockup backgrounds.

The final impression should be: **honest, precise, capable, welcoming, enduring, and quietly premium**.

## Kittl export checklist

- Export SVG for the production logo.
- Export transparent PNGs at 1024 px and 256 px.
- Export a 32 px favicon test.
- Confirm the icon is recognizable without the wordmark.
- Confirm the wordmark remains readable on the warm cream and dark walnut backgrounds.
